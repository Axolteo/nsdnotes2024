# Statistics: The what & why
**Probability** = $\frac{x}{n}$ where x is the preferred outcomes and n is the total outcomes.
$\bar{x}$ is the mean. 
## $\bar{x} = \frac{x_1 + x_2...x_n}{n}$
**Sample:** A representative portion of a population. It approximates the various demographic characteristics of a population.

# Scientific Method
Signs of a good article/study
1. Properly constructed
2. Cites their sources
3. Peer-reviewed
There are instances where things in a study can be flawed. 

Journalistic sources are often relevant in debate. 
**Pillars of Journalism:**
- Impartial
	- Is there inflammatory language?
- Objective
	- Is it factual?
- Balance
	- Are they giving both sides of an argument?
- Bias
	- Are they being obviously biased towards one side?
- Privacy
	- Do they respect privacy?
- Public interest
	- Is the thing they're writing about in the vested public interest?
- Timely
	- Is the topic timely?

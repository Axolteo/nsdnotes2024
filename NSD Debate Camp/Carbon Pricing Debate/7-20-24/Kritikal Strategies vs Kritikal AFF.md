# K NEG vs K AFF
It's all about the link. Prove why the AFF's construction/method is bad. Instead of starting from a theory of power, start with a point of disagreement (preferably several). Why does the AFF do something that is *bad?* Have a general idea of what you want your 2NR to say about why the AFF is bad. 
## Tell the judge:
1. **Where do we disagree?**
	1. Think about what the AFF said. Link arg has to be about the AFF. Where in the link do we disagree? Authors? Lines? Words? Explanations of history? Narration? Representing? 
2. **Why do we disagree?**
3. **Weigh it.**
Goal of NEG is to rejoin the AFF. 
## Explain the link in terms of the AFF.
Contextualize the impact to the AFF. If the AFF hasn't met their burden of proof, you should presume against AFF. 
How do you make a radical shift from the status quo? If the shift is marginal, then you should vote NEG because the amount of offense after given the opportunity to change the world doesn't have a reason to be rewarded with a pass. 
Tell the judge about presumption. It's your best friend when you're NEG. If you don't know what the AFF does, the judge probably doesn't know either. 
Don't get fear of presumption get in your way. 
## Alternatives
Your departure from the status quo and the AFF's method. Goal is to solve the links. A good alternative is one in which you can solve for the AFF and the AFF's offense. If you can explain your alternative in a way that solves everything, you basically win.
A floating pick is where in the first CX, you're just like "The alternative that could result in mechanisms that look like the AFF" and then you reveal that it was always a pick. Undercuts a lot of the AFF's abilities.

## Perms
When we're thinking about competition, perms are good. As NEG, beat perms by making sure our links are as specific to the AFF as possible. It then prevents the links from being solved by a permutation. If AFF talks about the world in a way that is factually incorrect and leads to a bunch of bad conclusions, and they say do both, then you can prove that the AFF was bad. Explain why it's bad that we disagreed to generate a disadvantage to the permutation.
The way you can prove that perms are illegitimate by pointing out parts of the AFF they have severed and things that are intrinsic that they've added in.
Talk about the tradeoff.
